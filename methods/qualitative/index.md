# Qualitative Approaches

Close reading and interpretive work, where the analyst's judgment about meaning does the heavy lifting. The four pages below cover methods I supervise in the BA and MA programs. Each one answers a different kind of question about texts and cases in political communication. Students often combine these methods with each other, and they routinely pair them with a computational method for sampling or validation. The [Computational & Quantitative Approaches]({{ '/methods/quantitative/' | relative_url }}) page covers the other side of the split.

## Combining qualitative and computational approaches

Many strong theses pair a qualitative method with a computational one. Common pairings include the following.

- **Framing analysis + topic analysis.** A topic model surfaces candidate frames in a large corpus, and you read closely within each cluster.
- **Discourse analysis + keyword-in-context tooling.** The computational side locates passages worth reading, while the interpretive judgment stays with you.
- **Comparative case study + descriptive corpus statistics.** Report aggregate measures such as volume and sentiment alongside your cross-case interpretation. Keyword prevalence can also help if it matches the research question.
- **Process tracing + digital archives.** Computational search helps identify causal-process observations you might otherwise miss.

The two sides work together. The [Computational & Quantitative Approaches]({{ '/methods/quantitative/' | relative_url }}) page covers the methods on the other side of the split.
